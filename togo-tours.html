Discover Togo

A journey with

Togo rewards travellers who want to experience a country closely. Start in lively Lomé, follow stories across Lake Togo to Togoville, or head inland for the cooler landscapes and waterfalls around Kpalimé. HOBTOURS can connect these places into a private day, a longer route or a wider West Africa journey.
