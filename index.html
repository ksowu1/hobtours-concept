Meet Africa differently

A journey with meaning.

HOBTOURS began in Lomé with one belief: the most memorable trips feel personal. Today, we create journeys across Africa that pair trusted local knowledge with the freedom to travel your way.

Whether you are returning to your roots, planning your first safari, chasing the coast or bringing a group together, we take care of the details from the first conversation to the journey home.

Meet your travel specialist

Africa is not one story. Every journey should reveal its richness.

Bernice Owoh · Founder of HOBTOURS

Born in Ghana to Nigerian parents and based in Togo, Bernice brings a naturally pan-African perspective to every journey. With more than six years in travel and tourism, she has personally guided over 100 travellers across the continent.

Her approach is warm, practical and deeply personal: understand the traveller first, then build the Africa experience around them.
