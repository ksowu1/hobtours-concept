Good to know

Before we
start planning.

Does HOBTOURS arrange travel across Africa?+

Yes. Tell us the country or region you have in mind—or ask us to help you choose. Every route is confirmed according to current availability and the arrangements possible in each destination.

Can my journey be private or designed for a group?+

Yes. We plan private journeys for individuals, couples and families, as well as group travel for friends, organisations and celebrations.

What can HOBTOURS arrange?+

Depending on your destination, we can support itinerary planning, tours, local transportation, airport services, accommodation, flights, travel insurance options and visa guidance.

How do I receive a quote?+

Complete the trip planner with your dates, group size and interests. Your request will open in WhatsApp so you can send it directly to HOBTOURS and continue the conversation.
