Good to know

Planning as
a group.

Can HOBTOURS plan a trip for a group we already have?+

Yes. Share your preferred dates, approximate group size, destination and interests. HOBTOURS can prepare a private itinerary and quote around your group.

How will hiking difficulty and safety be handled?+

Each confirmed activity should include the expected duration, terrain, fitness level, local guidance, transport arrangements and practical preparation information before booking.

When will dates and prices be available?+

Dates and prices will be published only after routes, partners and availability are confirmed. Registering interest helps HOBTOURS understand demand and contact you when a suitable trip opens.

Does registering interest reserve a place?+

No. It starts a conversation and adds you to the relevant update list. A place is reserved only after HOBTOURS confirms the trip and provides the booking terms.
