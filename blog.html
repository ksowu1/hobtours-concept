Gadao-Adossa: the festival of cutlasses

Gadao and Adossa are two major celebrations of Togo’s Tem community. Gadao gives thanks to the ancestors for the harvest, while Adossa honours warriors through the dramatic dance of cutlasses. The three-day gathering usually takes place in March, bringing memory, movement and community together.

Akwasidae: once every six weeks

On special Sundays, Manhyia Palace welcomes visitors for Akwasidae. Horns, fontomfrom drums, kete and adowa dance accompany a vivid display of Ashanti custom, leadership, kente and gold—an experience rooted in the bonds between chiefs, elders and community.

Fosse aux Lions National Park

In Togo’s far north, Fosse aux Lions brings together savannah, wetlands and acacia-dotted plains. The protected landscape surrounds Tandjouaré, a practical base for hiking and wildlife journeys in the Savanes Region.
